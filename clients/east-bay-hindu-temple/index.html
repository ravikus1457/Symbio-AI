Welcome · स्वागतम्

A temple built by the community, for the community

East Bay Hindu Temple is a non-profit place of worship serving Hindu families across Pittsburg and the greater East Bay. We are dedicated to keeping our traditions alive — through daily worship, the teaching of our scriptures, and joyful festivals that bring families together across generations.

Whether you are visiting for a quiet moment of darshan, booking a pooja for your home, or celebrating a festival with the whole community, you will find a warm welcome here. Everyone is welcome, regardless of background.

Darshan · दर्शन

Our Deities

Come and receive the blessings of the divine. Each murti is lovingly cared for and adorned daily. Bow your head, light a lamp, and feel at home.

Shri Ram Darbar

श्री राम दरबार

Bhagwan Ram, Mata Sita, Lakshman & Hanuman ji

Bhagwan Ram — the seventh avatar of Vishnu and hero of the Ramayana — embodies dharma: truth, duty, and righteous living. He is worshipped with Mata Sita (virtue and devotion), his brother Lakshman (selfless service), and Hanuman ji at his feet. Praying to the Ram Darbar brings harmony to the home and courage to walk the right path.

Shiv Parivar

शिव परिवार

Bhagwan Shiv, Mata Parvati & family

Bhagwan Shiv, the Mahadev, is the great transformer — worshipped as both cosmic yogi and loving householder. With Mata Parvati and their sons Ganesh and Kartikeya, the Shiv Parivar is the ideal divine family. Devotees offer jal and bel leaves, especially on Mondays and Maha Shivaratri.

Durga Maa

दुर्गा माता

The Divine Mother, worshipped through the year

The Divine Mother riding her lion is Shakti herself — the fierce, protective energy of the universe. She vanquishes negativity and evil, as in her victory over Mahishasura, while showering her children with a mother's love. Navratri's nine nights honour her nine forms.

Shri Hanuman ji

श्री हनुमान जी

Protector and devoted servant of Shri Ram

Hanuman ji is the embodiment of bhakti — devotion, strength, and humility — and the greatest devotee of Shri Ram. Reciting the Hanuman Chalisa is said to remove fear and every obstacle. Tuesday is his day: join our Tuesday 6:30 PM Chalisa & Ramayana Path.

Shri Ganesh ji

श्री गणेश जी

Remover of obstacles, worshipped first

The elephant-headed son of Shiv and Parvati is Vighnaharta — the remover of obstacles — and the lord of wisdom and new beginnings. Every pooja begins by invoking him. Offer modak and durva grass; Wednesdays and Ganesh Chaturthi are especially dear to him.

Radha Krishna

राधा कृष्ण

Divine love, devotion, and joy

Bhagwan Krishna — the eighth avatar of Vishnu, who gave the world the Bhagavad Gita — together with Shri Radha embodies the highest divine love and joy. Their worship through bhajan and kirtan fills the heart with sweetness, celebrated at Janmashtami and Holi.

Durga Maa and the Shiv Parivar show the temple's own murtis. The remaining niches show classic devotional artwork by Raja Ravi Varma (public domain) until each murti's photograph is added.

First Visit · प्रथम दर्शन

Visiting for the First Time?

Everyone is welcome, and entry is always free — come as you are. A few simple customs will help you feel at home in any mandir.

  • 👟 Remove your shoes Leave footwear at the racks by the entrance before going in for darshan.
  • 👘 Dress modestly Clothing that covers shoulders and knees is appreciated — traditional or casual.
  • 📱 Silence your phone Keep voices low in the darshan hall; please ask before photographing the murtis.
  • 🍚 Receive prasad Accept prasad with your right hand or both hands — it is a blessing to be shared.
  • 🪔 Join the aarti You're welcome to stand with everyone during aarti and offer the flame's blessing.
  • 🅿️ Parking is easy Free parking is available on-site and on School Street.
Do I need an appointment for darshan?
No — come any time during open hours for darshan. Appointments are only needed for personal poojas and ceremonies with Pandit ji, which you can arrange at (925) 812-0581.
Is there an entry fee?
Never. The temple is free and open to all, regardless of background. It runs entirely on the generosity of devotees — donations are welcome but never required.
Can I bring offerings from home?
Yes — flowers, fruit, and sweets are all welcome offerings. You can also purchase prasad and puja items at the temple shop.
I'm not Hindu — may I still visit?
Absolutely. Visitors of every faith and background are warmly welcome. Come for a quiet moment, ask questions, and stay for prasad.
Is the temple wheelchair accessible?
Please call ahead at (925) 812-0581 and we will gladly make arrangements to assist you.
