# Gokul Custom Signs — Website Pitch

**Prospect:** Gokul Custom Signs / Gokul Printing Press & Sign Service
**Location:** 5504 Florin Rd, Sacramento, CA 95823 · (916) 308-1607
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 10 AM–6 PM · Sat 11 AM–4 PM · Sun closed
**Current web presence:** No website. Directory listings only (Yelp,
Chamber of Commerce, Nextdoor, MerchantCircle) plus a Facebook page
(@gokulcustomsigns).

> Verify all details above with the owner before the meeting — services,
> hours, and spellings were pulled from public directory listings.

## The opening line

*"You make signs so people can find your customers' businesses — but when
people search for **your** business, there's nothing there. Right now, anyone
who Googles 'signs near me' in South Sacramento finds FASTSIGNS, Signs Now,
and Sacramento Pro Signs. You're invisible to them, and you're the closest
shop."*

## The problem (what we found)

1. **No website at all.** Searching "Gokul Custom Signs" returns third-party
   directories, not the business. The shop has no page it controls.
2. **Inconsistent listings.** The business appears under at least three names
   (Gokul Custom Signs / Gokul Printing Press–Sign Service / Gokul K Printing
   Press) across Yelp, Nextdoor, and Yahoo — which splits reviews and confuses
   Google Maps ranking.
3. **Competitors own the search results.** Every "custom signs Sacramento"
   search sends customers to national franchises with polished sites and
   online quote forms.
4. **The best services are hidden.** Wedding/engagement/bilingual invitation
   printing is a genuine differentiator on Florin Road — and it's mentioned
   nowhere a customer would find it.

## What we're proposing

A one-page website (the demo in this folder) that does four jobs:

1. **Get found** — proper title/description for "signs, banners, printing,
   Florin Rd Sacramento"; address, phone, and hours marked up so Google can
   read them; one canonical name for the business.
2. **Get called** — tap-to-call phone number in the header and hero, and a
   quote form that goes straight to the owner's phone/email. Every visit has
   exactly two exits: call or request a quote.
3. **Show everything the shop does** — signs, banners, t-shirts, business
   cards, invoice books, copies/fax/notary, and a dedicated wedding &
   invitation cards section for the community trade.
4. **Answer the #1 question instantly** — a live "Open now / Closed now"
   badge computed from the shop's real hours, plus today's row highlighted in
   the hours table.

## Benefits to lead with in the pitch

- **Be the answer, not the runner-up.** A website with the right keywords +
  a cleaned-up Google Business Profile puts the shop on the map for "signs
  near me" searches in 95823 — searches that today go to franchises.
- **Quotes while you sleep.** The form collects name, contact, job type, and
  details 24/7. A walk-in shop with 10–6 hours currently loses every customer
  who shops at 9 PM.
- **One link for everything.** Facebook posts, Yelp, business cards, and the
  van can all point to one address that the owner controls — no more relying
  on whatever Yelp decides to show.
- **The wedding-card trade, finally visible.** Bilingual wedding and
  engagement invitations are high-margin, community-driven work. A dedicated
  section makes the shop *the* place on Florin Rd to search-and-share for it.
- **Zero maintenance, near-zero cost.** Static site — no plugins, no
  updates, no monthly platform fee. It can host for pennies and the open/
  closed logic updates itself.
- **Looks the part.** The design uses the sign trade's own language — big
  poster lettering, enamel red and marigold, layered "vinyl" shadows — so the
  site itself is a sample of what good signage feels like.

## Suggested upsells (phase 2)

- Google Business Profile cleanup + review-collection card at the register
- Photo gallery of real jobs (before/after storefronts, banners, shirts)
- WhatsApp quote button (fits how a lot of the invitation-card trade
  communicates)
- Simple Spanish / Hindi–Fijian language toggle for key sections
- Symbio AI chat widget to catch and qualify quote requests in real time

## Demo notes

- `index.html` is fully self-contained (no build step, no external assets).
  Open it in a browser, or send the hosted preview link.
- The quote form is a front-end demo — production wiring would post to the
  owner's email/SMS.
- Dual theme: renders in light (print-stock white) and dark (ink navy) based
  on the viewer's device setting.
