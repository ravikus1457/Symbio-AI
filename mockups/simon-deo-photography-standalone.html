About

It started with a yearbook and a Canon 7D

Simon picked up his first DSLR in his senior-year yearbook class and never put a camera down again. What began as photographs of family and friends grew into a profession built on the same idea: the best pictures happen between people who trust each other.

Based in Sacramento, Simon photographs weddings and celebrations across Northern California and beyond — with a love for natural light, natural backgrounds, and the in-between moments that families talk about for years.

“Clients become friends. That’s when the real photographs happen.”

Coverage

The Day

After

Entrances, speeches, the first dance and the wildest ones. Four to five hours, edited fast enough to relive it while it’s still fresh.
