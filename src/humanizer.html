🧠 Deep rewrite with a local AI (optional, free) ▾

The cleanup above is instant and needs nothing installed. For a deeper rewrite — one that re-shapes sentences, not just swaps words — you can run a free AI model on your own computer with Ollama. It still never sends your text anywhere online.

📘 How to install a local AI (one-time, ~10 minutes) ▾
  1. Go to ollama.com/download and install Ollama for your computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux). It is free and open-source.
  2. Open a terminal (on Windows: Command Prompt) and download a small writing model: 
    ollama pull gemma3:4b
    On an 8 GB laptop gemma3:4b or qwen3:4b-instruct-2507 work well. With 16 GB, gemma3:12b is noticeably better.
  3. Ollama runs quietly in the background from then on. Come back to this page and click Re-check — your model appears in the list.

If it says “blocked by the browser”

That happens only when you open this file directly (a file:// address). It is a one-line fix: let the model accept requests from this page, then restart Ollama.

  • Windows: search “environment variables” → Edit environment variables for your account → New → name OLLAMA_ORIGINS, value * → OK, then quit & reopen Ollama.
  • Mac: in Terminal run launchctl setenv OLLAMA_ORIGINS "*", then quit & reopen Ollama from the menu bar.
  • Linux: sudo systemctl edit ollama.service, add Environment="OLLAMA_ORIGINS=*" under [Service], then sudo systemctl restart ollama.

The easiest way to avoid this entirely: serve the file over localhost. In the folder with this file run python3 -m http.server 8080 and open http://localhost:8080/humanizer.html — no setup needed.

An honest note. This tool makes writing sound more natural — it is not a “beat the AI detector” button, and no honest tool is. Detectors like Turnitin are unreliable and often flag genuine human writing, but reordering words does not reliably fool them, and passing AI work off as your own can carry real consequences. Use this to polish your own drafts and to keep evidence of your writing process (draft history in Google Docs or Word), not to disguise authorship.
💡 What it changes (and what it never touches) ▾

It rewrites

  • Overused AI words (delve, leverage, tapestry, pivotal, robust…) → plain synonyms.
  • Stock phrases (“it's important to note that”, “plays a crucial role in”, “in today's fast-paced world”) → trimmed or dropped.
  • Formulaic sentence openers (Moreover, Furthermore, In conclusion…) → softened or removed.
  • Tell-tale structure: em-dash pile-ups, stacked hedging (“may potentially”), “not only… but also,” participle tails (“…, highlighting its importance”), assistant leftovers (“Certainly!”), curly quotes and stray invisible characters.
  • Optionally, contractions in Casual voice (“do not” → “don't”). Off in Formal.

It protects

  • Anything in “quotation marks” (direct quotes stay word-for-word), code, URLs, and email addresses.
  • Your meaning, facts, names, and numbers — it changes wording, never claims.

Not sure about an edit? Turn off Highlights to read it clean, or just retype any word — it is your draft. English only for the instant cleanup; other languages should use the local-AI deep rewrite.
